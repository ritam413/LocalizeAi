# Six-Pager Workflow

Execute the **Six-Pager** strategy memo generator for the specified proposal.

1. **Activate Skill:** Load and apply `six-pager` structure.
2. **Draft Sections:**
   - 1. Context & Background
   - 2. Non-Obvious Guiding Tenets
   - 3. Problem Definition & Quantified Impact
   - 4. Proposed Solution & Architecture
   - 5. Strategic Alternatives Matrix
   - 6. Anticipated FAQs (Internal & External)
3. **Deliver Document:** Produce a narrative memo with high signal density.
