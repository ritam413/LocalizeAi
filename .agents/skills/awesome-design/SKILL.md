# Awesome Design (DESIGN.md Catalog & Brand Systems)

Use this skill when designing or redesigning applications with high-fidelity brand design systems, importing `DESIGN.md` specs from the **awesome-design-md** catalog, or generating custom cohesive design systems.

## Quick Brand Design Systems Catalog

Fetch raw `DESIGN.md` specifications from `VoltAgent/awesome-design-md`:
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/main/design-md/<brand-slug>/DESIGN.md`
