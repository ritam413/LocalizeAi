# Humanizer: Remove AI Writing Patterns

Rewrite AI-sounding text so it reads like a real writer, not a chatbot. Keep what it says. Do not make anything up.

## Key AI Tells to Eliminate

1. **Staging:** Signaling importance instead of stating facts; artificial contrasts that only add weight (`not X but Y`); one-line dramatic closers (`That is the real win.`, `Let that sink in.`).
2. **Rhythm by rule:** Triads (`fast, scalable, and secure`) and dashes applied mechanically everywhere.
3. **Inflation:** Ordinary facts dressed as pivotal or expert-backed.
4. **Formatting by rule:** Bold text and title casing applied mechanically to every item in a list.
5. **Leftovers / Residue:** Chatbot greetings (`Certainly!`, `Of course!`, `Great question!`, `I hope this helps!`) or knowledge cut-off disclaimers.

---

## Workflow

1. **Mark the tells:** Identify structural formulas and patterns.
2. **Draft the rewrite:** Preserve 100% of facts, metrics, and meaning while eliminating AI tells.
3. **Check the draft:** Ensure sentence lengths vary and sound conversational and human.
4. **Output:** Return the humanized prose directly.
