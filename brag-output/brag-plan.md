# Brag Plan: LOCALIZE

## What is this app?
LOCALIZE is an autonomous 7-agent AI post-production crew for film and video localization that turns any finished video and target culture prompt into synchronized, character-consistent cinema dubs with automated QA defect self-repair.

## The angle
Hollywood-grade cinema localization without the assembly-line garbage. Most tools do a blind translation, slap robot audio over video, and pretend it fits. LOCALIZE runs a full autonomous post-production room: story analysis, idiom adaptation, character voice cloning, millisecond atempo sync, and closed-loop QA continuity repair.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep cinema black with a single glowing declaration:
"MOST AI DUBBING TOOLS ARE BLIND."
Followed by a shattering transition into the studio engine.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **7 Autonomous Post-Production Agents**: Real-time pipeline orchestration (Story Analyst, Localization Director, Voice Director, Sync Engineer, Subtitle Director, QA Continuity Agent).
- **Millisecond Speech Window Alignment**: Precision `atempo` time-stretching & syllable expansion fitting exact actor mouth windows.
- **Closed-Loop QA Self-Repair**: Detecting subtitle drift and audio overflows, surgically repairing defective segments without rerunning the entire movie.

## Outro / punchline
"THE NEXT ERA OF WORLD CINEMA IS AUTONOMOUS."
**LOCALIZE**
Film Localization. Mastered.

## User flow worth showing
1. Drop raw film & target language prompt.
2. Watch the 7-agent crew analyze scenes, preserve idioms, and synthesize character voices.
3. Observe the QA Continuity Agent detect an overflow and trigger a real-time self-repair loop to perfect the cut.
